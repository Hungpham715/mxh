## 3. Stratification

Each type is split into 3 frequency tiers × 2 evidence-source classes = 6 strata; allocation is proportional to
stratum size (largest-remainder rounding). Inside a stratum, edges are sorted by (source_id, target_id) and shuffled
with the type's seed; round 1 takes the first n_h, round 2 the next n_h' (proportional to what remains).

Frequency = number of corpus papers on which the institution appears (via affiliations.jsonl). Tiers are cut at the
1/3 and 2/3 quantiles of the per-edge frequency within the type.

| Type | Frequency key | Tier cut points (low ≤ a < mid ≤ b < high) |
|---|---|---|
| AFFILIATED_WITH | papers of the target institution | 4, 14 |
| ORG_COAUTHORED_WITH | min(papers of the two institutions) | 1, 3 |
| ACTIVE_IN | papers of the source institution | 6, 13 |

Evidence-source class ("confidence" proxy; no numeric confidence score exists in the pipeline):
- `acl_backed`: the edge is supported by an affiliation that agrees with the affiliation printed in the ACL Anthology
  metadata (AFFILIATED_WITH: any evidence authorship; ORG_COAUTHORED_WITH: two different authors on one paper;
  ACTIVE_IN: at least 2 evidence papers).
- `openalex_only`: otherwise (arXiv papers, or ACL authors without a printed affiliation).

## 4. Review sheet

- `evaluation/review_sheet_round1.csv`: UTF-8 with BOM, all fields quoted, 150 rows, 21 columns.
- Rows are sorted by `paper_id` after sampling. Each row is anchored on the evidence paper that is shared by the most
  sampled rows, so 150 rows need 103 distinct anchor papers; total supporting papers listed = 284
  (AFFILIATED_WITH 56, ORG_COAUTHORED_WITH 57, ACTIVE_IN 171), 35 rows anchored on arXiv papers.
- The sheet contains only source metadata: titles, links, author position and printed name, the affiliation printed in
  ACL Anthology metadata and the raw OpenAlex affiliation string. It does **not** contain automated labels, the stratum,
  the `affiliation_check` flag or any score.
- `evaluation/review_sheet_round1_overlap_reviewer2.csv`: the 30 `is_overlap=1` rows for an independent second
  reviewer.
